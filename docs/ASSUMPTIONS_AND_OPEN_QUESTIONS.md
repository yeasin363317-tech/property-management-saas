# Assumptions and open questions
Assumptions made without confirmation: default currency BDT and timezone Asia/Dhaka (editable per workspace); free plan limits 2 properties / 10 units / 3 team members; paid plans have no price until an admin sets one; invoices due day capped at 28; invitations delivered by sharing a code (no email); registrations open by default; one tenant record can link to one portal user.
Open questions: official Vibe 96 requirements; real pricing and billing provider; late-fee policy; tenant data deletion/anonymization policy and retention period; whether managers may see other managers' tenants (currently yes within the org); email provider; MFA policy; legal review of Terms/Privacy; market validation of the product hypothesis.
Admin capabilities needing special security review before real use: platform role grants, sole-owner suspension override, manual subscription changes, viewing org-level data in admin lists. Impersonation is not implemented on purpose.
